Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Last updated: January 2026

1. What Data We Collect

When you use Workout Tracker, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and securely hashed password
  • Workout Data: Exercises performed, sets, reps, weight, quality ratings, and energy levels
  • Usage Data: Timestamps of workouts and app interactions

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide workout tracking and logging functionality
  • Generate workout analysis, personal records (PRs), and progress insights
  • Display your workout history and calendar
  • Authenticate your account and secure your data
  • Send password reset emails when requested

3. What We Don't Do

✓

We do NOT sell your data. Your workout information is never sold to third parties.

✓

We do NOT share your data with third parties except for essential infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, database services) required to operate the app.

✓

We do NOT use your data for advertising. There are no ads in this app and your data is never used for marketing purposes.

✓

We do NOT track you across other websites. We don't use third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

4. Security Measures

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data:

  • Password Security: All passwords are securely hashed using industry-standard algorithms (PBKDF2 with SHA256). We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Encrypted Connections: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Managed Infrastructure: Data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure with security best practices.
  • Access Controls: Your workout data is only accessible to your authenticated account.

5. Your Rights

📥

Data Export: You may request an export of your workout data by contacting us.

🗑️

Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

✏️

Data Correction: You can edit or delete individual workouts and sets through the app interface.

6. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For questions about this privacy policy, data requests, or concerns, please contact the app administrator.
