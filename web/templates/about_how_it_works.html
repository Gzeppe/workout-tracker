How Workout Tracker Works

Welcome to your comprehensive workout tracking companion! This app is designed to help you log, analyze, and optimize your fitness journey with data-driven insights.

Key Features

Intelligent Workout Analysis

After every workout, the app provides you with comprehensive feedback including:

  • Personal Records (PRs) - Instantly see when you've set new records for max weight, max reps, or highest volume for any exercise
  • Improvement Tracking - Compare your current workout to previous sessions and see exactly where you've improved
  • Areas to Improve - Get specific recommendations on exercises that need more volume or intensity
  • Performance Streaks - Track consistency with quality workout streaks
  • Context-Aware Motivation - Receive personalized encouragement based on your energy level and performance

Advanced Rest Timer

Stay on track with the integrated timer featuring:

  • Stopwatch mode - Track how long you've been resting
  • Countdown mode - Set 1-4 minute rest intervals
  • Persistence - Timer keeps running even when you add sets
  • Audio alerts - Beep when countdown finishes

Complete Exercise Library

Access a curated library of exercises organized by muscle groups, with options for different equipment and variations.

HIIT Timer

Configure custom interval training sessions with adjustable work/rest periods and audio cues at each transition.

Getting Started

  1. Start a Workout - Click "Start Workout", give it a name, select your workout day and energy level
  2. Log Your Sets - Select an exercise, enter weight and reps, rate the quality of each set
  3. Use the Timer - Track rest periods between sets to maintain optimal intensity
  4. End and Review - When done, end your workout to see your analysis and any new PRs
  5. Track Progress - Check the calendar to see your workout consistency over time
