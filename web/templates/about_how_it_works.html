How Workout Tracker Works

Welcome to your comprehensive workout tracking companion! Track weightlifting, cardio, and HIIT workouts with data-driven insights to optimize your fitness journey.

Three Workout Modes

🏋️

Weightlifting

Track sets, reps, weight, and quality. Get PR detection and intelligent analysis.

🏃

Cardio

Log duration, distance, speed, and incline for running, cycling, and more.

⚡

HIIT

Built-in interval timer with presets like Tabata. Audio cues included.

Key Features

Menu-Based Dashboard

Quick access to everything from one place:

  • Start Workout - Choose your workout type and begin tracking
  • Previous Workouts - View history with list and stats views
  • Calendar - See your activity and schedule future workouts

Workout Templates

Save time with reusable templates:

  • Create templates for any workout type
  • Save completed workouts as templates
  • Quick-start with one tap

Smart Calendar

Plan and track your fitness schedule:

  • View completed workouts by date
  • Schedule future workouts
  • Start workouts directly from scheduled items

Intelligent Workout Analysis

After every weightlifting workout, get insights including:

  • Personal Records (PRs) - See new records for weight, reps, or volume
  • Improvement Tracking - Compare against previous sessions
  • Areas to Improve - Get recommendations for exercises needing attention
  • Context-Aware Motivation - Encouragement based on energy and performance

Built-in Timers

Stay on track during workouts:

  • Rest Timer - Stopwatch and countdown modes for weightlifting
  • HIIT Timer - Full interval timer with work/rest phases
  • Cardio Timer - Running duration tracker for cardio sessions
  • Audio alerts when intervals complete

Getting Started

  1. Choose Your Workout Type - Select weightlifting, cardio, or HIIT from the Start Workout menu
  2. Configure Your Session - Set up based on workout type (exercises, cardio type, or HIIT intervals)
  3. Track Your Progress - Log sets, intervals, or cardio metrics as you go
  4. Use the Timer - Rest timer for weights, interval timer for HIIT
  5. Review Your Results - End your workout to see the summary and any achievements
  6. Save as Template - Optionally save successful workouts for quick reuse
