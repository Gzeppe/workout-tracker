Training Suggestions

Recommended Training Split

For optimal results, we suggest following a structured body split routine:

Day 1: Upper Body

Focus on chest, back, shoulders, and arms

Examples: Chest & Triceps, Back & Biceps, Shoulders & Traps

Day 2: Lower Body

Target legs and glutes

Example: Legs day

Day 3: Core

Strengthen your foundation with abs and core work

Example: Core day

Day 4: Upper Body

Return to upper body with different exercises

Rotate through different muscle groups

Pro Tip: This alternating split allows adequate recovery time for each muscle group while maintaining workout frequency. Use the "Combination" day option when you want to mix exercises from multiple categories!

Maximize Your Gains

1.

Use the Built-in Timer

Rest periods of 2-5 minutes between sets are ideal for hypertrophy (muscle growth) and strength building. The app's timer helps you stay consistent.

2.

Track Quality & Volume

Rate each set's quality to get personalized insights. The app analyzes your volume and quality to identify trends and opportunities for improvement.

3.

Celebrate PRs

Every personal record is automatically detected and highlighted in your workout summary. Track your progress over time!

4.

Progressive Overload

The app's analysis helps you identify when to increase weight or reps, following the principle of progressive overload for continuous gains.

5.

Listen to Your Body

Log your energy level honestly. The app provides different feedback based on whether you're feeling exhausted or energetic - both are valid training days!
